About me

I’ve spent 8+ years across engineering and technical operations. Some of my work starts with a developer reporting a broken tool. At Ubisoft, I joined team standups to find problems people hadn’t filed, turned their feedback into bug reports, and verified fixes with engineering.

At SSI Schaefer, I helped customers troubleshoot warehouse software and wrote guides they could use themselves. At Maxis, I took a similar approach to recurring internal questions. I built a Slack intake that offered relevant documentation and gave urgent issues a route to a person.

I enjoy figuring out how systems work and why they break. I like troubleshooting infrastructure, automating repetitive steps, and connecting tools when doing so makes someone's job easier.

As a Claude Certified Developer, I've built and tested AI-assisted self-service support. I also explore LLM integrations, AI agents, Model Context Protocol (MCP), local AI infrastructure, and workflow automation in my own projects. I'm interested in where AI can improve productivity while preserving reliability, security, and human oversight.

As a former part-time professor at St. Lawrence College, I delivered 100+ hours of technical instruction. Teaching shaped how I explain technical decisions, mentor others, and work through problems with a team.

I value curiosity, accountability, simplicity, and continuous learning. I take time to understand the problem, choose tools that fit, and build systems people can depend on. I'm interested in work that brings engineering, AI, automation, and technical support together to solve practical problems.

Outside work, I build home labs, automate things around the house, explore new technology, watch anime, and collect gadgets. I'm based in Barrie, Ontario, Canada.
